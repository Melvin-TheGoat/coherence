The pills scroll and the valley does not. The list fades at both ends rather than stopping on a hard line, so it reads as sitting in the scene instead of on a panel laid over it. Nothing else moves, which is why this feels like one screen changing its mind.

 the rule this sets

The pill is the same object as Sound and Silence notifications one screen back: cream at 94 percent, radius 16, one soft shadow, and the 2pt gold outline for chosen that the profile fields and onboarding answers already use.

Gold lands twice: the chosen pill and Done. The guided card is warm rather than gold because it is content, not a choice you have made, and its badge is trace, because a lock is neither chosen nor achieved.

Group labels sit on the sky in the valley's ink, quiet, because the pills are the content and a heading on a white bar would rebuild the sheet.

The tones keep their number in the icon slot. The frequency is what they are and it costs no extra room.

No play buttons. Tapping previews and selects, which is what the rows already did; three sage bars say which one is sounding.

One copy fix that is not cosmetic. Silence read "Just the measurement, or your own audio". A phone sit measures nothing, so that is false for every session started here now. It reads "Just you, or bring your own audio".

If this is the vibe for the whole app, the thing to copy is not the painting. It is the four rules under it: one scene per job and it does not move; every control is a cream pill floating on that scene; Otto says the sentence that would otherwise be a title; and exactly one gold object per decision. Home, Profile and Friends can each take that without each needing a landscape of their own.
