The repo's own fourteen-app research, applied: no boxes, bare text on hairlines, and who can see it first, because that decides whether a description is even worth writing. Denser, and it reads more like a form.
Three things to decide with the screen: (1) Photos. Friends posts today need a front-camera selfie, no library, which was Aziz's rule so a post is always you, meditating. Strava lets you add anything. Keep the selfie rule for Friends and allow any photo for Only you? (2) A title. Strava has one and our feed card shows one. Worth a line, or one field less? (3) Feeling scale. Five faces here; the old screen had a number out of ten. Faces are faster and carry no arithmetic.
What is already gone: the Save session screen no longer opens after a sit, and the results screen with its "How did it feel?" card no longer opens either. Until one of these is built, a session can still be filled in from its row on Profile.
