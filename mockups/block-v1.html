Melvin, 2026-09-21: 808 is about consistency. The Guide tab becomes Block (the guide moved to a circle under the streak on Home, already built). You pick when you want to meditate and which apps pull you away; Otto holds them in that window until you have sat. Opening one shows Apple's shield, "Ask Otto" sends a notification, and tapping it opens 808 to one of twenty ways Otto asks you to sit first. Every one ends in the same two doors: meditate now, or ask him for 5 to 60 minutes. The full brief is CONSISTENCY.md.

4. Decided (Melvin, 2026-09-22)

  1. Meditating releases the apps for the rest of the window, so someone can meditate twice a day.
  2. "Not now" costs Otto only if the whole window then passes without a meditation, and then 20 × hours held / 24: a skipped Mindful day costs 20, a 3 hour window 2.5. Coming back ten minutes later costs nothing.
  3. Strict (no "Not now" at all) ships in the first version.
  4. Block is paid.
  5. The default is Mindful day: the apps you pick are held all day until you meditate, and one switch turns it off.
  6. A free person finds Mindful day waiting on the Block tab, and switching it on opens the free-week offer.

Before Block can ship: Apple approves Family Controls (Distribution) for the app and each of its three extensions. The steps are in CONSISTENCY.md. I can build and test on a phone meanwhile.
