Not here: your scores chart, your awards, the sessions you kept to yourself, your private notes, and every heart, breath and stillness reading.

Drawn from `PersonView`, not imagined. Name, handle, since, follow counts, and for friends the streak, post count and average score, then your posts. The dashed line lists what is not there, and it is the list the chips on your own page promise.

Why the chips say what they say

A post carries the free share card and nothing measured, which `test_postCarriesOnlyTheFreeCardFields` locks: score, minutes, streak, technique, title, sound, caption, photo. No heart, breath or stillness, ever, for anyone. So "Only you" on the scores chart is literally true, and so is the last line of the friend's page.

Awards are marked Only you because that is the truth today, not because it is the right answer. They are the most postable thing in the app and they are not shared. Worth a decision: making the shelf public is a small change to `Profile` and would give the Friends tab something to show on a day nobody posted.

With Friends off in Release, the chips and the follow line do not draw. There is no public anything, so a page explaining who can see what would be answering a question the build does not raise.
