What the Watch took with it

The awards shelf is 15, not 18. It landed at 50, Deep at 75 and Rare air at 90 are score awards, and with no Watch they can never be earned, so leaving them on the shelf puts three permanently unreachable squares in front of somebody through no fault of theirs. They are filtered the way "Brought a friend" is filtered when Friends is off. `AwardEngine` keeps them, so a wrist session still earns them.

Line by line: the scores chart is minutes; each session row shows its duration where the score was; a friend's Avg score is Practised; posts lose their score line.

Amber still means the measured quantity of a session, which in the baseline is its length and with a Watch is its score. That is why the bars keep it rather than moving to sage: the colour's meaning did not change, only the instrument. Blush still carries the streak, and the one gold object on the page is an earned award.

Nothing is deleted from the engine. `MeditationStats`, the score, the three awards and the curves all still exist and still fill in for a wrist session. This is the baseline's page, not a smaller product.
