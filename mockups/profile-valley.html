What changed, and why each one

The 84pt circle and the three stacked lines are gone. A big empty avatar over a centred name is a settings screen's idea of a profile. Identity is one pill: portrait, name, handle, and when you started, with Edit as a quiet gold word rather than a bordered button.

Four stats stay in one pill and keep blush, because the streak is the blush colour everywhere else in the app. Amber stays reserved for measured scores, which is why the bars and the session numbers are amber and these are not.

The gear is a cream circle on the sky, the same material as every other control. It was floating unattached over the gradient.

Otto is the meditating pose here, not the greeting one. He is sitting in your valley rather than waving you into a session: this page is not a door to anything.

One idea I did not build, and why

The meadow could grow with your practice, one flower per session, so your valley visibly fills in. It is honest (a count, not a claim), it costs nothing (the flower field is already a list), and it is the kind of thing people screenshot. It does not work on this screen: the content covers the meadow from the first scroll, so nobody would see it. It belongs on Home, where the scene is the whole screen.
