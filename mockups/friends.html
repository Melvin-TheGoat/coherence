The Search tab becomes Friends. Mutual friends only (you ask, they accept), a feed of what your friends posted, one reaction per post, no comments in v1, no counts shown to anyone but you. A post is made from the results screen and carries exactly what the free share card carries: score, minutes, streak, technique, a photo, a caption. Never a heart-rate or breath number, never a curve. Decisions to confirm

  • Tab name. "Friends" replaces "Search". The icon is two people.
  • The reaction word. "Respect" was cut (Aziz). "Nice sit" is the new placeholder: it is what meditators actually say to each other after a session. Other options: "Well sat", "Deep", "Still", or a plain 🙏 with no word at all.
  • No comments in v1. Reactions carry the loop; comments add the moderation surface Apple audits hardest. Add them in v2 if people ask.
  • What a post carries is fixed by two rules (5.1.3 and the free tier) and is not a design choice: score, minutes, streak, technique, photo, caption. No heart-rate or breath numbers, no curves, even for paid users.
  • Reward: free users get 10 sessions of full evidence per friend who accepts and sits once (stacking, capped at 50 outstanding). Paid users get the "Brought a friend" award and the Circle card skin. Nothing for the invited person: Apple rejects that.
  • Backend: CloudKit public database, no server. Report emails come through an Apps Script like the waitlist. Photos are screened on device with Apple's Sensitive Content Analysis before upload.
  • Ships as 1.1, after 1.0.2. Flips the age-rating answers for UGC and Social to Yes, adds privacy labels, a terms section and a policy section.
