The score on a post

A session the Watch measured carries one, a session run on the phone carries none, and the card drops the capsule and the column rather than printing a zero. What is left to decide is whether it is also a choice, and where that choice lives. Below: the card both ways, then three homes for the switch.

A stance, not a decision per sit. Nothing to weigh after a session, and no day-to-day signal: friends see scores from you always or never. The cost is that it is invisible at the moment it applies, so somebody may not remember it is on.
What I would do: option 1 now, option 3 if it is wanted later. The score is already optional in the only way that matters, which is that a session without one posts fine. A per-post switch (2) is the one I would avoid: it makes every post a small announcement about the number that is missing, and it adds a decision to the screen at the moment somebody just wants to be done.
If the score is going away entirely, as the pivot suggests, this whole question answers itself: the card keeps time, streak and technique, which every session has, Watch or not.
