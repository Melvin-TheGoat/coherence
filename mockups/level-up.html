Otto changes form

It is always Otto. As you meditate, he is reborn into new forms: a red panda, a tiger, a phoenix, a dragon. Each form has its own seven glow states. This page shows the moment he changes, the screens around it, and what Melvin decided.

The moment Otto changes form

Pokémon's evolution, Duolingo's confetti, Finch's card, played as one sequence. It plays after the reward screen's Continue, the slot the award unlock uses today, so it never stacks with points and glow. Tap anywhere to skip to the card.

About 10 seconds. The card waits for a tap.
  1. Night fallsThe valley darkens and the stars come out. Otto's bubble clears.
  2. "Otto is changing..."He turns into a glowing white silhouette.
  3. Silhouette flickerHe flickers between his old shape and his new one, faster and faster.
  4. FlashA warm white flash, eased in fast and out slow.
  5. The new formDay returns. He pops in, color first, light rays turning behind him.
  6. ConfettiA burst from where he sits, in 808's six colors, falling with real gravity.
  7. The card slides upHe rises to make room. The card says what happened and that he starts at Steady.

Why it works. The flicker gives the change its weight, the confetti makes it a celebration, and the card brings it back to 808: calm, honest, nothing to chase. All of it is SwiftUI over stills that already exist, so it ships without new Rive rigs.

What's decided

Otto's form follows lifetime points earned, never the balance. Points are spent on hats, so a balance can go down. Lifetime points only climb, so spending in the Shop never slows his next form. Points stay as they are: 1 per whole minute meditated, plus 10 per week finished with a friend. Hand-logged sessions earn none.

Still open for Melvin and Aziz

  1. The app icon. Does it follow the chosen form, or stay the sloth? Following needs alternate icons in the build (one per form), and iOS shows a system alert every time an app changes its icon. Recommended: the icon stays the sloth, since it is what people know 808 by.
  2. Glow when switching back. Each new form starts at Steady. When you switch back to a form you unlocked earlier, does it show your current glow, or the glow it had when you left it? Recommended: your current glow, because glow is your consistency, not the form's, and one number is easier to understand than five.
