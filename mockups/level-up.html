Companions evolve

Everyone starts with Otto. Points carry you along a line of five companions, each with its own seven glow states. Six ways to play the moment a new one arrives, the screens around it, and a recommendation.

The rule, recommended

Evolution reads lifetime points earned, never the balance. Points are spent on hats, so a balance can go down. If evolution followed the balance, buying a hat would push your next companion further away, and the Shop would feel like a penalty. Lifetime points only ever climb. The Shop and evolution read the same earnings but never compete.

Points stay as they are: 1 per whole minute meditated, plus 10 per week finished with a friend. Hand-logged sessions earn none, so evolution can only be reached by meditating in the app.

Example thresholds At 15 minutes a day: Red panda in about 5 weeks, Tiger in about 3 months, Phoenix in about 9 months, Dragon in about 22 months. At 30 minutes a day, half that. The gaps widen on purpose so the first arrival comes early and the Dragon means something. For scale, hats cost 20 to 1,000 points today.

Earlier companions stay. Reaching the Tiger does not take Otto away; it adds the Tiger to your collection and you choose who meditates with you. Recommended over a straight replacement because people get attached to Otto (he is the app icon, the widget and the onboarding), and replacing him would feel like losing something you earned. It also gives the collection screen a reason to exist.

Recommendation

A then B, in one sequence. The morph plays the moment (about five seconds, at dusk, tap to skip), and it ends on B's card in daylight, which says what happened and that Otto stays. A gives the evolution its weight, B keeps it 808: calm, honest, nothing to chase. Both are SwiftUI over the stills that already exist, so it ships without waiting on Rive rigs for four new creatures. Use C's path as the Profile card rather than as the moment itself. Skip D (loot box read) and F (eggs only suit two of the five).

Rules to go with it: lifetime points drive evolution; earlier companions stay; glow belongs to the person and carries to whoever is on the cushion; the arrival plays once, after the reward screen, never mid-session.

Open questions for Melvin and Aziz

  1. Thresholds. 500 / 1,500 / 4,000 / 10,000 are examples. The Dragon at about 167 hours is close to two years at 15 minutes a day. Is that the right length for the last one, or should it come sooner?
  2. Can you switch back? Recommended yes, any time, from Companions. The alternative (the newest always sits) is simpler but takes Otto away.
  3. Does glow carry over? Recommended yes. Glow is the person's consistency, not the creature's, so a new companion arrives at the same stage. Starting each one fresh would punish reaching it.
  4. Do hats carry over? Every hat is fitted to Otto's head across 13 looks. Options: hats stay Otto's only; each creature gets its own fitted set (large art cost); or hats unlock per creature over time. Recommended for v1: hats stay with Otto, and the Shop says so on a creature without them.
  5. Is it still "Otto"? Pokémon copy says "Otto grew into a Red panda", which suggests one character changing. A collection suggests new friends. Pick one so the copy agrees everywhere: Otto is the name of the first companion, and each new one has its own name.
  6. Existing members. Lifetime points can be rebuilt from stored sessions, so someone with 30 hours already would unlock two at once on update. Play them in order, one after the other, or one combined arrival?
  7. App icon, widget and Watch. Do they follow the chosen companion, or stay Otto? The widget and Watch draw stills, so following is cheap; the icon would need alternate icons.
  8. Hats spent before this ships. Lifetime points include spent ones, so nobody loses progress for having shopped. Confirm that is the intent.
