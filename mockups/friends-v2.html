808 · Profiles, and saving a session

Strava is the layout guide; 808's dark ground and gold stay. Three changes: (1) your nickname and your @username are separate, and the username plus a profile photo are made in their own step at the end of onboarding; (2) people who already have 808 get a one-time "Friends is here" prompt that asks for the same; (3) a session no longer drops you back at Home. It ends on a Strava-style Save session screen where you name it, add a photo, and choose Friends or Only you.

Decisions to confirm

  • Save session shows the score before you save. Strava shows distance and time on its save screen, so the number is there before you choose. The alternative keeps the big reveal for Results by hiding the score until after Save, at the cost of choosing to share a session you haven't seen.
  • Default visibility: Friends for anyone with a profile (Strava defaults to Everyone). Only you would be the cautious default; Friends is what makes the feed fill up.
  • Username is required, with one exit. You asked for it to be required, and the prompt has no skip. The exit is needed because a phone with no iCloud account cannot save a username at all, and blocking the app there would lock people out. Review risk, stated plainly: guideline 5.1.1(v) says apps may not force account creation for features that don't need one, and a required public profile in a meditation app is close to that line. If it's rejected, the fix is one switch that adds "Not now" to both screens and keeps the username required only for Friends.
  • Private notes stay private. Today's reflection note becomes "Private notes" and is never posted. The public text is a separate "How did it go?" description, so nobody publishes their journal by accident.
  • Nothing measured is posted, even on Friends: score, time, streak, sound, title, description, photos. Heart rate, breathing and the curves stay on the phone (guideline 5.1.3 and the free tier).
