What I changed and why

The intro paragraph is gone. It said sessions run open-ended and that audio from another app keeps playing. The first half now lives on the Ready screen, which is where somebody is deciding, and the second half is the one line Otto says. A sheet that opens with two sentences of explanation is a settings page.

Play buttons removed, one indicator added. Twelve identical grey circles were the loudest thing on the screen and every one of them did what tapping the row already did.

Not changed: the ordering rules (nature by familiarity, brainwave deepest first, tones low to high) are derived from the catalog and stay exactly as they are, so adding a preset still needs no UI edit.
