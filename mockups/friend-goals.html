Four kinds: a nudge from a friend, a friend meditated, a Sunday heads-up only if the week is still open, and the week result. Each can be switched off in Settings.

Decisions to make before building

  • Which goal types? Shown: "days each" and "minutes together". Days each is simpler and fairer when one person meditates longer.
  • How many shared goals at once? Duolingo allows up to five friend streaks. Suggest the same.
  • What counts as a day? Suggest any session of 5 minutes or more, matching what opens Block's apps. Sessions logged by hand ("Record one") don't count, or either person could fake their half.
  • If the week is missed, the streak goes back to 0 for both. Suggest one free "pause week" per pair every couple of months, for holidays and sick weeks.
  • Nudge limit: once a day per friend, and never between 10 pm and 7 am.
  • Build note: works on the CloudKit public database Friends already uses, no server. A nudge is a small record addressed to your friend, and CloudKit pushes the notification. Progress comes from sessions only, never from Screen Time.
