# Otto breathes with his arms: art brief (2026-09-23, Aziz)

For onboarding's one-breath screen: Otto standing, raising his arms overhead on
the in-breath (4 s), holding (2 s), lowering them on the out-breath (4 s). Rive
rotates each arm at the shoulder, so the art has to come in separate parts.

References: `otto-v3/otto-wave.png` (his only standing pose; ignore the raised
arm and the yellow marks) and `otto-v3/otto-sit.png` (his closed-eyes face).

## What to deliver: three PNGs

1. **Body**: Otto standing, facing straight at the viewer, **no arms**. Head,
   torso, legs and feet. Eyes gently closed, small calm smile. Where the arms
   would join, finish the shoulders with fur so there is no hole.
2. **Left arm** (his left, the viewer's right) alone: straight, hanging down
   at his side, hand and three claws at the bottom, a **rounded shoulder end**
   at the top so it can turn without showing a hard cut.
3. **Right arm**: the same, mirrored.

## Rules

- **All three on the same canvas size**, 1024 x 1024 or larger, square.
- **Each arm drawn exactly where it attaches.** Stacked in order (arms behind
  or in front of the body, either is fine, say which), they must look like one
  normal standing Otto with his arms relaxed at his sides.
- **Transparent background.** No ground shadow, no glow, no outline, no marks.
- **Same style, fur texture, colours, lighting and proportions** as the
  reference. Same size head. Front-facing and symmetrical.

## If separate parts come out wrong

Fallback: three full images of the same standing Otto, same canvas and
position, only the arms changing: **arms relaxed down**, **arms straight out
to the sides**, **arms straight up overhead, palms together or open**. Rive
switches between them in short fades. It works, but it is choppier.

## Prompt

> The same cute sloth character as the attached reference (brown fur, cream
> face mask, dark brown eye patches, round dark nose, soft 3D storybook style).
> Standing upright, facing the viewer, symmetrical, eyes gently closed, small
> calm smile, feet planted. Deliver three separate PNGs on identical 1024x1024
> transparent canvases, aligned so they overlay perfectly: (1) the sloth with
> NO arms, just head, body and legs, shoulders finished in fur; (2) its left
> arm alone, straight and hanging down in the exact position it attaches, with
> a rounded shoulder end and its three-clawed hand; (3) its right arm alone,
> mirrored. Same art style, colours, lighting and scale as the reference. No
> background, no shadow, no outline, no extra marks.
