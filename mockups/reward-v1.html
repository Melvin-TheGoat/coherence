After a session: the reward

What plays the moment a session ends, replacing today's glow burst on Home. It borrows the structure of Duolingo's lesson-complete (one card at a time, each number counting up, then the streak) and Clash Royale's rewards (coins flying into your bank one by one, a level-up burst), in 808's valley. It runs about nine seconds, every beat has a haptic, and a tap anywhere skips to the next beat, so it is never in the way. Press Play.

0.0sThe landing. The valley brightens from behind him, soft rays turn slowly, and Otto hops (squash, lift, land). "Session complete!" pops in with a bounce, the minutes under it. A heavy haptic on the land.
1.2sThe cards, one at a time (Duolingo). Minutes counts up from 0 with a light tick per step. Then the streak card: the flame flares and the number rolls from yesterday's to today's with a bump. Then points.
3.2sThe coins (Clash Royale). Points count up on the card while coins arc one by one from it into the bank at the top right; the bank ticks up with each one landing, and each landing ticks. One point per minute, so 18 coins, capped at a quick 18 flights however long the session.
5.6sThe glow. Otto's glow bar fills from before to after, with a shimmer running along it; +10% floats up off it. If he crosses into a new look, the level-up: a flash, he changes into the new look with a burst, and a gold ribbon names it ("Otto reached Steady"). Success haptic.
8.0sContinue. The green button rises. An award earned by this session opens right after, on the existing award screen. Then Home, with the "Add how that felt" toast as today.
What changes in the app: this becomes a full-screen cover that opens when a session lands, instead of the glow playing on Home. The numbers are all real: minutes, the streak before and after, points (1 per minute), the glow before and after. Nothing is invented. Hand-logged sessions play it too, but without coins, since they earn none. Sound is optional: a soft chime per coin like Clash Royale's would suit it, and it stays silent when the phone is on silent.
