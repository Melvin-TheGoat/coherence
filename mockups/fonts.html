808 uses SF Rounded at every size, which is the right first move and is also what every friendly iOS app reaches for, so the app currently sounds like all of them. Below is the same slice of Home set in four faces. Judge them on the small text as much as the headline: a display face that is charming at 22pt and mushy at 12 will cost more than it buys, because most of the words in this app are small ones.

What I would ship, and it is a split. A display face for the big text and SF Rounded for everything under about 15pt. The reasons are boring and they decide it: SF is hinted for these screens at small sizes and a web face is not, it carries every weight and every language 808 ships in, and it scales with Dynamic Type for free, which a fixed-size bundled face does not. The headline is where a face has room to have a personality, and the headline is about eleven strings.

Nunito is the safe pick: rounded terminals, a real personality at 22pt, still perfectly readable at 12, and it has the weights we use. Fredoka is the closest to Duolingo and the biggest swing; it is lovely large and it gets doughy small, so it would be headline-only without question. Quicksand is the most "wellness app" of the four and the lightest, which fights the fat shapes everywhere else. Baloo 2 is chunky and warm and its lowercase is a little wild at small sizes.

Licensing: all four are SIL Open Font License, which permits embedding in a commercial app with no fee and no attribution in the UI. The licence file ships in the repo. Bundle cost is roughly 40 to 120 KB per weight, and we would take two.
