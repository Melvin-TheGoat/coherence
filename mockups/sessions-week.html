Sessions, a week at a time

Aziz: "the list but you see it based off a weekly basis and then theres a calendar option to change the specifc week seamlessly"
Direction A's rows, scoped to one week, with two ways to move: the arrows step a week, and the label opens a calendar that picks one. Weeks run Monday to Sunday and the current one is called "This week" rather than its dates.

You pick a WEEK, not a day. The whole row lights, tapping anywhere in it selects it, and the sheet closes on the tap. A dot marks a day you practised, so the calendar answers "which week was that?" before you have chosen anything. Days outside the month are dimmed but still belong to a selectable week.

Four words and no advice. An empty week must not be scored, coached, or apologised for: it is a week you can see because you asked to see it, and the arrows are right there. The summary line is absent rather than reading "0 sessions · 0 min", which is a scoreboard of nothing.

Underneath, a past week: once the week is not this one, rows say their date in full, because "Friday" is ambiguous the moment you are looking at a week that is not the current one.

Moving between weeks

GestureWhat happens
ArrowsOne week, the list sliding in from the side you came from. The header's label and summary cross-fade rather than sliding, so the card does not appear to change size.
Swipe the cardSame as the arrows, same animation. Cheap to add and it is the gesture anybody will try first.
The labelOpens the calendar. Picking a row closes it and the list arrives from the direction of travel, so jumping back three months still reads as going backwards.
From HomeTapping a day on Home's week strip opens Profile at THAT day's week with the day's rows in place, instead of today's filter, which is what it does now.
Why a week and not a month. A week is the unit this product already thinks in: the streak's rest day is one per seven, Home's strip is seven days, and a week of sessions fits on one screen with no scrolling, which a month never does. The header's two numbers then mean something you can act on.
One thing to decide, because the repo has a standing rule against it. CLAUDE.md says 808 has no month view anywhere, and `MonthCalendar` was deleted on purpose: a grid of dots answers "did I show up" at a resolution nobody needs, and it drew days that had not happened yet as blanks. The sheet here is a PICKER, not a status display, which is a different job: it exists to answer "which week was that", it is only on screen while you are choosing, and it selects rows rather than days. I think that clears the rule. If you would rather not have a grid at all, the alternative is a list of weeks ("Sep 14 – 20 · 5 sessions") which is honestly easier to scan and carries the counts, but it is not a calendar and you asked for one.
An implementation note, so the animation does not get designed twice. The obvious way to build swipeable weeks is a paging `TabView`, and it is the wrong tool here: a paging view forces every page to one height, and a week of one session is a fifth the height of a week of six. The card stays a single view whose CONTENTS change, with the slide drawn as a transition and a drag gesture that commits past a threshold. Same look, no fixed height, and it still lives inside the page's own vertical scroll.
