Where "Meditate together" starts

Two buttons, same result. On the Friends tab, "Meditate with a friend" is the one people see; it asks which friend first. On a friend's profile, "Meditate together" skips that step. Both open the same setup screen, which we'll design next. Sections D and E below show the Friends tab after you've started a goal, and the goal's own page. The orange dashed outline only marks the new button for this review. Example people and numbers.

I · One session counts for every goal

You meditate once and it counts for every shared goal, your personal streak, Otto's glow and your points. The reward screen says so. Sessions under 5 minutes or logged by hand still count for your own streak, but not for shared goals.

I · After a session

A small pill under "Session complete!": "Counted for Ana and Daniel 🤝" with their faces. One goal reads "Counted for Ana"; three or more, "Ana, Daniel and 1 more". It doesn't show if the session didn't count for any goal. Tapping it opens Friends.
