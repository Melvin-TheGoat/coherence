This changes a decision

Since Sept 29 the trial is hidden until someone says no (Melvin's call): the paywall sells Monthly and Yearly with no trial, and the free days only appear on the first rung of the ladder. Every option here puts the trial up front instead. Worth a word with Melvin before I build.

What stays the same

  • Prices and products: $7.99 and $29.99, the 3-day trial plans that already exist.
  • The trial length is read from App Store Connect, so "3 days" changes everywhere if that does.
  • "No, I don't want to pay" still leads to the half-price rung.

What building it takes

  • The reminder itself: one notification, the day before the trial ends. Today nothing in the app sends it.
  • B2 asks for notifications here. iOS only asks once, so the reminders screen after the paywall would change to match.
  • Dates are worked out from the day of install. The mockups show Nov 11, today.

App Review

  • A and B start no purchase, so "free" and "$0.00" can lead there. The paywall keeps the price as the biggest number.
  • The timeline's "unless you cancel" is the same promise the footnote under the button already makes.
