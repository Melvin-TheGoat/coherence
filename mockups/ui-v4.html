808 UI v4: fewer things, the same app

Melvin, 2026-09-20: a total UI overhaul, aim for simplicity. This is the proposal, drawn before any Swift, per the standing rule. Nothing here changes what 808 measures or how it scores; it changes how much of the app you have to hold in your head.

The thesis, and every cut below follows from it: 808 is one loop. Sit, see the number, come back tomorrow. A screen either serves that loop or it is furniture. Today the loop is spread over five tabs and roughly nine blocks of Home and results; the same loop fits in four tabs and four blocks.

1. Four tabs, not five

Guide is eight articles behind a permanent tab. It is read once, near the beginning, and it is most useful in the half second before you sit rather than as a destination you visit. It moves into the plus sheet, where the question "what do I actually do?" is already being asked.

Cut: the Guide tab. The eight methods move to a "How to meditate" row inside the plus sheet and a link in You. Nothing is deleted; `GuideView(embedded:)` already exists and already opens the setup sheet.
Kept: the plus. It is still the only way to start a session and still the only amber object on the bar.
Costs. The tour anchors `.guide` at the Guide tab item, so the walkthrough needs one edit. And anyone who opened Guide to browse now has to go through the plus, which is one more tap for a page most people read once.

2. Home is today, and nothing else

Home currently carries the greeting, the date, Otto and his line, the streak, a seven day strip and three full session cards, each with a score, a title, a duration and three metrics. Profile then shows the streak again, four stats, a score chart and the same sessions. Two screens answering the same question is the single biggest thing to fix.

Cut: the Recent list. Three tall cards become one line for the last sit: the number, when, how long, what it was. The rest of the history is one tap away in You, where it is not competing with today.
Cut: three metrics per card. Heart, Still and Breath on a Home card ask you to read three numbers before you have read the one number the app exists to give you. They stay on the results screen, where they belong.
Kept: Otto, the streak, the seven days. Otto is the voice, the streak is the reason to come back, and the rolling seven days answers "did I show up" for the days still winnable. Four blocks, one screen, no scrolling on a phone this size.
Open question. With Recent gone, the only way to reach an old session from Home is through You. If you would rather keep one tap, the last line can push straight to that session and the rest still lives in You.

3. Results: the number, then the evidence, then you

Results is the best screen in the app and the longest: ring, "how is this scored", verdict, technique and sound, three metric tiles, a resonance chip, three full graphs, the photo, the reflection card and the share row. Everything below the verdict is evidence, and evidence is something you ask for.

Three rows and a button. Sound is one row that opens the four worlds, rather than a grid plus a list. The guide is the third row, in the moment it is useful.
You is where everything historical lives: the score chart, the full log, awards, the four stats, settings. Unchanged except that it is now the only place those live, so nothing is drawn twice.

What this is not

Not a repaint. The friendly redesign is three days old and it works. Same palette, same Baloo 2, same Otto, same cards. This is subtraction only.
Not a change to the score or the evidence. Nothing measured moves. The curves, the tiles and the verdict are all still there, one tap deeper.
Not a Friends change. The feed was rebuilt for air on 09-18 and is the one screen already doing this. It gets the tab it has.
Three decisions before any Swift:
  1. Guide loses its tab, yes or no.
  2. Home keeps the last sit as one line, or keeps a short Recent list.
  3. Save session merges into results, or the two screens stay.
Answer those three and the build is roughly a day, with the tour anchor and the first-session paywall hook as the two things to re-test.
