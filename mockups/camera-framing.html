The live screen. The same camera session, not restarted, shrunk to a thumbnail so it reads as "the camera is on", never as a readout. A teal hairline means the recorder has fixed its region of interest. No numbers, no outline, no live biometrics: evidence comes after.

Decisions in this mockup

  • One camera session, owned by the coordinator. The sheet starts it on appear, hands it to the live screen at Begin, and releases it if the sheet is dismissed without beginning. Recording (t = 0) still starts on the Watch's started-ack, exactly as today; the preview phase before it records nothing.
  • The figure fixes the region of interest early. If the person was found in the seconds before the Watch starts, that median box becomes the ROI from t = 0 instead of thirty seconds in. If not, the in-session detection runs as before, so the capture files keep their shape.
  • Colour grammar. white: an instruction, not yet met. teal: the body's signal, found. gold: Begin, and only Begin. The plan's earlier "turns gold when framed" is retired; a found torso is guidance, not an achievement.
  • The outline is a Shape, not an image. Drawn from paths (head, shoulders and arms, crossed legs, the shins crossing), like the logo, so it scales with the preview and takes any stroke colour.
  • Sizing. The preview keeps 3:4 and takes the width left after the title, the subtitle and the Do Not Disturb tip: full content width on a large phone, a little narrower on a small one, never scrolling and never pushing Begin off the screen.
