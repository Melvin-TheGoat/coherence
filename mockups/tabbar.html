Profile. Identity on top (initials avatar, the name from onboarding, practising-since), the four stats, an awards strip, the full log. Settings in the gear. The month picker leaves Journey; the calendar lives on Home.

Decisions in this mockup, and two questions

  • Calendar on Home only. Journey's month picker goes away; Profile keeps the stats, awards and the full log. Tapping a dotted day on Home opens that day's sessions, which is what the picker was for.
  • Begin button gone. The gold plus is the one gold CTA on the bar, and the colour rule (one gold thing per section) holds because the tabs are grey.
  • Question: the name. The app has a display name from onboarding ("What should we call you?") and no usernames. A handle like @melvin is new data, needs uniqueness, and needs a server the app does not have. Proposal: initials avatar + display name now, handle when the friends feature brings a backend.
  • Question: Home's greeting. Keep "Good afternoon, Melvin" on Home, or move the name to Profile only and let Home open on the streak?
