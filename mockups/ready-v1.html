Ready when you are

The screen you meet before every sit. Otto is seated in the daylight version of the valley you are about to be in, so tapping Begin reads as arriving rather than switching screens. Below him are the only two decisions, and the one instruction that has a deadline.

Read this before you judge the notifications row. iOS gives apps no way to turn on Do Not Disturb. There is no API, and the private App-prefs: deep links are a rejection. So a switch that silently puts you on DND cannot be built, by us or by anyone. What can be built is on panel 2, it is genuinely automatic once it is set up, and it takes the user about forty seconds one time. The row is drawn as a switch on purpose: after the setup it behaves like one.

Let's make this automatic

Apple doesn't let 808 switch on Do Not Disturb by itself. It lets you switch it on for 808, once, and then it happens every time.

What I need from you before building it

One decision and one warning.

The decision: is the Shortcuts route worth it? It is the only thing that actually silences a phone, it works, and it costs a one-time setup screen plus a Shortcuts trip that some people will bail out of. The alternative is to cut the row entirely and keep the one-line Control Center tip that is on the screen today. I would build it, because "nothing will interrupt" is half of what this screen is promising and a tip is not a promise.

The warning: the row must never claim more than it is doing. If somebody skips the setup, the switch cannot sit there looking on. Panel 1 is the un-set-up state on purpose, and tapping it goes to panel 2 every time until it is real.
