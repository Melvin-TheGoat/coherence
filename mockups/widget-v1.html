Four calls before I build it

1. Small, medium, or both? My pick: both. Small for people who want Otto and nothing else, medium for the line he says.
2. Does it show for people who haven't paid? My pick: yes. A lapsed member seeing Otto fade on their home screen is the best reason to come back, and it shows nothing they haven't already earned.
3. What does a tap do? My pick: open Home, where Otto is. A second option is a "Meditate" button on the medium widget that opens the plus screen.
4. When does it ship? 1.1 is in review, so this goes in the next update (1.2), not this one. Adding it now would mean pulling 1.1 from review.
