808 · Save session v6: the one decision the research surfaces

Two reference passes are now in, across fourteen apps (Strava, Hevy, Strong, Gentler Streak, Nike Run Club, Whoop, Oura, Apple Fitness, Letterboxd, Calm, Headspace, Balance, Day One, Bevel, Flighty, Things 3), most of them read off real screenshots at pixel level. Everything in v5 survives except one question, and it is the one that decides how clean the screen reads: does a text field live inside an outlined box, or bare on a hairline? Strava on iOS boxes it. Nearly every app admired for clean design does not. You chose Strava, so v5 is drawn boxed. This page shows the same screen both ways so you can choose with the evidence in front of you.

My recommendation

  1. B. Both passes, fourteen apps, and the one that matches our situation best (Letterboxd: you just finished something, now grade it) all land on the same rule: do not box a field. Strava is the outlier, and its own users call the save screen cluttered. Strava's iOS behaviour stays exactly as you chose: Cancel-style exit top left, nothing top right, the filled button pinned at the bottom. What changes is only the skin on the inputs.
  2. If you want A, everything in v5 is already drawn that way and the Swift is a few more lines. No argument; it is a legitimate reading of "copy Strava".
  3. Either way the camera is unchanged from v5, and nothing here touches what a post carries.

One finding from the meditation apps worth keeping in a drawer, not for this screen: Calm's post-session check-in has no buttons at all. A question, a 3 by 4 grid of mood tiles, and tapping one is the whole interaction. If we ever add a "how do you feel" step after the score, that is the shape.
