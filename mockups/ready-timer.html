A timer on the Ready screen

Today every session is open-ended. This puts the length back on the Ready screen as a clock you can see and a gesture that sets it, in the valley's own colors. The number is the same clock the sit screen shows once you begin, so what you set here is what you then watch count down. Scroll all three: they are live. Open (the ∞ end) keeps today's behavior, so nobody is made to choose.

1  the tape RECOMMENDED

A ruler you slide under a fixed needle. One tick a minute, a label every five, a soft tap on every tick. Ticks swell as they near the needle and the big clock rolls to the new number. It reads like a timer at a glance and flicks from 5 to 45 in one swipe. It sits in the empty sky, so Otto, his line and the pills do not move at all.

2  the drum

The number itself scrolls. Drag up or down on it and the minutes roll past like an odometer, the neighbours tilting away above and below. The biggest, most tactile of the three, and the most honest about what it is: you are scrolling the time. The cost is that nothing says "drag me" until you touch it, so it carries a small hint line.

3  the ring

Wind the sit screen's own ring. Drag around it like a kitchen timer; the amber arc is exactly the arc that will shrink while you sit. The strongest link to the next screen, but a circle is fiddly to drag with a thumb, one lap is sixty minutes, and it takes Otto's line out of its spot above his head.

How it behaves, whichever one is picked

  • Tap the clock to type an exact length (added after review): the clock becomes a number field with a Done pill; 0 is Open, 600 minutes at most.
  • Range: Open, 1 to 60 minutes in single minutes, then 75, 90 and 120.
  • It remembers your last choice (the stored default length, which Settings already edits), so a daily 10-minute sitter never touches it.
  • A timed session ends itself at zero: the phone already knows how, the sit screen's ring counts down, and the sound stops with it. Open still ends when you tap End.
  • A soft tap on every minute while scrolling. Nothing buzzes once the session starts.
  • Under 5 minutes does not open Block's apps. Nothing on this screen says so; worth deciding whether it should.
