808 · v7: a photo for every sit, and a calendar that shows them

Aziz, 2026-09-15: "you should still have an option to take pictures after the meditation even if its a private one and then those pictures can be shown in the calendar." Yes. This is the best idea in the thread, because it changes what the selfie is. Until now it was a toll you pay to share. Now it is your own record of having sat, and sharing is one more thing you can do with it. BeReal's most-loved feature is exactly this: Memories, a private calendar of your own shots. The mandatory-for-Friends rule stops feeling like a gate once the photo was for you first.

Home. A day with a photo shows the photo, 24 by 26, where the dot was. A day you sat without one keeps its dot. Rows gain a small portrait thumbnail before the chevron; a row without a photo is exactly as today. The month becomes a strip of your own face, which is a stronger "look how much you sat" than twelve gold dots.

The decisions underneath it

QuestionAnswer, and why
Optional or required?Optional for Only you, still required for Friends. The BeReal rule is about what you put in front of other people. Your own calendar can have gaps.
Front camera only, still?Yes. Same camera for both. If the private camera could flip, a back-camera shot taken privately could be shared later by changing visibility from the results screen, and the "you, meditating" rule would break silently. One camera, one rule. If you want the view from the cushion as well, that is a second photo and a separate decision.
Where does a private photo live?A new SwiftData model, SessionPhoto, in the synced store: session id, the JPEG (1080px, about 200 KB, external storage) and a 200px thumbnail for the calendar. It rides the existing private iCloud sync, so photos survive a new phone the way sessions do, and account deletion removes them with everything else. Health data stays where it is; a photo is not health data. Cost: one more record type to promote Development to Production before release, which is already a gate on the checklist.
What happens when a private session is shared later?The same photo is uploaded to the public database as the post's picture, as today. Switching back to Only you takes the post down and leaves your copy alone.
Does it appear anywhere else?The results screen shows it above the reflection card, where the session's own record lives. Not on the share card yet: a photo would make the free card far stronger, but that is a card redesign and its own mockup.
Privacy policyOne line in the private-iCloud section, both copies: photos you take after a session are stored with your sessions and are shared only when you post them to friends.
