808 · Save session, Strava-shaped, and an in-app camera

Two screens, from Aziz on 2026-09-15: "take a look at the strava UI for sharing a session and copy that, i dont like the UI here for after the meditation" and "i want the camera thing to be like an in app photo thing similar to sweatmates and bereal". Everything else he asked for that day is already built and on the branch: the phone opens on this screen when you pick it up after a sit, silence is a technique you can log, and the keyboard has a Done key.

1 · What today's screen gets wrong

Not the content. The content is right, and it is already in Strava's order. What is wrong is that every single field is a filled rounded box on a dark ground, so the screen reads as five heavy slabs with an uppercase label floating above each one. Strava does almost none of that: it puts text straight on the background and separates things with hairlines, which is why a screen with ten rows on it still feels light.

TodayStrava, and this proposal
Score, time, streak and sound inside a filled card.The numbers sit on the background, divided by hairlines. The result is the loudest thing on the screen, which is the point of it.
Title and both notes are filled boxes with an uppercase label above.Title is just large text you tap. The label is the placeholder. One hairline under it.
Technique and visibility are a menu button and a segmented control, styled differently from each other.Both are list rows: name on the left, current value on the right in grey, chevron. Identical shape, so the eye stops reading them one at a time.
The selfie is a 320pt block that only appears once you pick Friends, so the page jumps under your thumb.A photo strip near the top, always there, one tile wide until you add one. Adding a photo never moves anything below it.
Save is a full-width gold bar pinned to the bottom, competing with the content.Save is the top right of the nav bar, where Strava puts it and where a form's commit belongs.

3 · The camera, in the app

Today the selfie opens Apple's camera sheet. It carries Apple's chrome, a photo library button, and a preview step that says "Use Photo" in Apple's voice, and it looks like it belongs to a different app, because it does. The BeReal move is that the camera is the app for those few seconds: full-bleed preview, one shutter, nothing else competing.

4 · What I would build, and what I want you to decide

  1. Build: the three screens in section 2 and the two camera screens in section 3, keeping every rule already agreed. A selfie is still mandatory for Friends, still front camera, still no photo library, and the store still refuses a Friends post without one.
  2. Decide: does Save move to the nav bar? Strava puts it there and this mockup copies that. The cost is that it is a smaller target than today's gold bar, on the screen we most want people to finish. I lean Strava, since the screen is short enough that the bottom bar is not buying much.
  3. Decide: private notes inline or its own screen? Strava pushes to a separate screen, which is what the row above implies. Inline is one fewer tap and is what you have today. I lean inline, expanding under the row, because a private note after a sit is a thing people abandon if it costs a navigation.
  4. Decide: more than one photo? Strava takes many, BeReal takes one. The strip is drawn for many; the store, the feed card and the share card are all built for one. Staying at one is free; going to several is real work in the record and the card.
  5. Not doing unless you say so: the dual front-and-back capture in the third camera screen.

Nothing here changes what a post carries. Score, minutes, streak, technique, title, sound, description, photo, exactly as today, and never a heart rate, a breath rate or a curve.
