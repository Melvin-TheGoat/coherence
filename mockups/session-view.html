A session, opened · and what "This week" could be

Two things for review before any Swift. First, the screen a saved session opens on from Home's Recent or from Profile: today it opens the edit page. It should open a page that shows the session, the way a Friends post used to, but with everything you kept (all the photos and videos, how it felt, what you did, your notes, and the measurements if a Watch was on), with Edit one tap away. Second, options for the "This week" strip on Home, from the research on what other apps put there.

2 · "This week" on Home

What the research found (sources in the chat): most apps show a week as one mark per day (Duolingo's checkmarks, which is what we have), a weekly ring (Calm), bars of minutes (Apple Health's Mindful Minutes, Apple Fitness) or a plain count (Strava's Weekly Snapshot). Only a stack per day shows how many times you sat each day. Every option here is the same rolling seven days ending today, sample week: 9 sessions, 101 minutes, 6 of 7 days, Tuesday the forgiven rest day. Nothing compares against last week: Strava users call that guilt.

A · a cairn a day
This week9 sessions · 101 min
One stone per session, stacked; a longer session is a wider stone. Three sits make a small cairn, an empty day is bare ground, the rest day is a leaf. Counts at a glance, on theme (the cairn was the first streak icon idea), and it reads as building, never as a score.
B · minutes, one block per session
This week9 sessions · 101 min
Apple Health's bars, split. Height is minutes, and each session is its own block, so you see both how long and how many. The most exact, and the most like a chart.
C · a garden
This week9 sessions · 6 of 7 days
Each day is a plant that grows a leaf per session and flowers at three. Uses the clay sprout and flower you already made. Softest of the four; counts past three are harder to read.
D · Calm's ring
This week101 min · 6 of 7 days
Seven segments, one per day you sat, the week's sessions in the middle. Clear total, but it no longer says which days, and it is one more ring on a Home that already has Otto's glow bar.
My pick: A, the cairns. It answers "how many times" per day, which was the ask, without turning Home into a chart, and it is ours (stones, a quiet valley) rather than Apple's bars or Calm's ring. The header line keeps the plain totals (sessions and minutes), so nothing is lost. If you'd rather it be exact about minutes, B.
