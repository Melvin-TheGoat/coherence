Get comfortable, in the valley

The five-second countdown between Begin and the sit. Today it washes the whole valley out to cream and puts a brown number on it, so the one moment before somebody closes their eyes is the least 808-looking screen in the app. All three keep the valley and never cut to a different screen. They play on a loop; each ends where the sit screen begins.

A  the clock counts RECOMMENDED

Nothing new appears; things leave. The pills slide down into the meadow, the tape fades, and the big clock in the sky counts 5 to 1 in its own place and face. Otto's line becomes "Get comfortable." At zero he settles from waving to sitting and the clock becomes the session's own. Cancel is one cream pill where Begin was.

B  the ring fills

The sit screen arrives at once, and its ring is the countdown. The amber arc fills once over five seconds with the number inside, then the ring empties into the session's clock. The most seamless hand-off, but the Ready screen's clock disappears the instant Begin is tapped.

C  Otto counts

He says the numbers. His bubble holds a big 5, 4, 3, 2, 1 with "Get comfortable" under it, as if he is counting you in. The most character, but a speech bubble is a small place for the one number somebody reads with their eyes closing.
