Getting people to actually meditate

Two fixes Melvin and Aziz asked for on 12 October: every new member blocks at least one app during onboarding, and reminders go from one fixed daily ping to a few smart ones on days you haven't meditated. Below: what the research says, the decisions that need you, and the screens that change.

Decisions, all approved

1Taper for people who've gone quiet, instead of sending them more. You asked to increase reminders for people who really aren't meditating. Duolingo does the opposite: it sends less after a few silent days, then a "these reminders don't seem to be working" message, then stops. Its former CPO says sending more needed CEO approval, because people who switch notifications off during an aggressive test stay off forever. Recommend: taper, with a farewell on day 10.
2Up to three a day while someone is active. Your ask was two to three. Duolingo appears to cap at two (a routine reminder plus the evening streak saver), though that figure is from a teardown, not from Duolingo. Built as three: your time, a follow-up four hours later, and an evening last call. Dropping to two is one line.
3The app pick is required, but only after Screen Time access is granted. App Review 5.1.1(iv) and 5.1.2(i) don't let us require a system permission to use the app, and a 2023 rejection was for exactly an onboarding permission screen with no way past it. So someone who taps "Don't Allow" on Apple's prompt still gets through. Everyone who allows it must pick at least one app. Recommend as drawn below.
4Ask existing members once, on Home. Everyone who skipped Block during onboarding will never see onboarding again, and they're the people not meditating. Recommend: Otto asks once on Home, "Pick one app for me to hold", which opens the same screen.
5Show their real top apps: now or later? Apple lets 808 display someone's most-used apps with minutes, but never read them. The view is drawn by a separate extension and nothing can leave it, so it can't preselect anything; they still tap the app themselves. It needs a fourth Block extension, a Family Controls request for its App ID (Melvin, in the portal) and testing on a real phone. Recommend: ship the required pick now, add top apps in the next build.

Fix 2

Reminders that stop the moment you meditate

  • Today: one notification, repeating at the same time every day, including days you already meditated, with the same sentence every time.
  • New: only on days you haven't meditated. Meditating takes back the rest of that day's reminders, and nothing buzzes mid-session.
  • The message is learned, per phone. Each topic (streak, Otto's glow, held apps, your goal, the next hat, a gentle nudge) is one arm of a small bandit, like Duolingo's: a topic followed by a session within two hours gains weight, and a topic used recently waits its turn. Onboarding answers set where it starts: "I lose motivation" leans toward streak and glow, "my phone pulls me away" toward held apps.
  • Everything stays on the phone. They're local notifications, learning is stored on the device, and nothing about Screen Time leaves it.

Every line is checked by a test: no em dashes, no length that opens Block's apps, never telling someone what they lack, and true on the day it fires (the streak and glow are worked out for that day before the message is chosen). Friends get a topic once friends can see each other's day (backlog).

The reminder screen in onboarding

Was: "One nudge, at your time." and "One reminder a day at the time you pick…"

Becomes: "Nudges at your time." "A reminder at the time you pick, and a couple more on days you haven't meditated yet. They stop as soon as you do." The rest of the list (session chime, come-back heads-up, Otto when asked) stays.

Changing the words is required, not cosmetic: the old ones would be untrue.
