What has to be true before this ships

  1. It reverses Melvin's call. On 2026-09-29 he made the trial an upsell nobody sees until they decline ("we want them to not know it exists"). This leads with it. Both of you need to agree.
  2. No price or product change. It sells the existing trial products (yearlytrial, monthlytrial). The ladder's first rung (the trial) becomes redundant, so "No, I don't want to pay" goes straight to half price.
  3. App Review 3.1.2. The billed amount stays the most prominent price: $29.99/yr is the biggest price on C, "3 days free" is a chip, and the renewal line sits under the button at a readable size. Sweatmates' last screen (giant FREE, price in grey small print) is the pattern that gets rejected.
  4. The reminder has to be real. 808 sends none today. Build a local notification for the day before the trial ends. Screen B asks for notifications, which moves the existing reminder permission screen earlier. If someone declines, C drops the reminder row rather than promising it.
  5. Lifetime has no trial, so it leaves this paywall; it stays available from the downsell or Settings.
  6. Sweatmates' partner screen has an honest 808 version only if you turn on Apple Family Sharing for the subscription in App Store Connect ("one membership covers your family"). Not drawn; that one is a product decision.
  7. Otto's Day 1 / A week in pair uses his real glow stages, which is how the app actually works. Dates on C are computed from today.
