The header becomes a scene

Same gradient and same curved bottom as Home, so the two tabs are obviously one app, and the avatar centred at 86pt with a white ring so it reads as a portrait rather than a list row's icon. Everything that identifies you sits under it in one column: name, handle, since, friends. Nothing is left-aligned beside a thumbnail any more, which is what made the old header feel like a settings screen.

Four stats become one object

They were four separate rounded cards with gaps between them, which is four objects to say one thing. One card, four columns, hairline dividers. Streak and longest read in blush and sits and hours in amber, because that is the grammar the rest of the app now uses: blush is the streak, amber is what you scored and did.

The month comes back, and this is a fix

When Home took the week strip I wrote that the month "lives on Profile". It did not. Profile never had one, so the month calendar was orphaned in the codebase and a person lost the only view of more than seven days. It belongs here, with the arrows that let you walk back through it, which is exactly the month picker that was removed in September for appearing twice. It appears once now.

Awards get a card and some size

The shelf was loose on the background at 54pt with 9.5pt labels. On a card, at 62, with the unearned ones in the empty-slot tone rather than a dimmed version of the real thing. An unearned award should look like a space for something, not like a faded copy of it.

What stays off

Otto's chat row and the "next award" progress bar, both cut by Melvin on 2026-09-18 and both still correctly cut. Otto's door is the results screen, where the question has a subject, and a progress bar on a screen that already shows the shelf is one more thing undone.
