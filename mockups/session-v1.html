The session screen

Every other screen in this app is Duolingo. This one is the practice, and it is the only place the product has to shut up. It also carries a constraint nothing else does: the timer only counts while the app is open, so the screen is ON, in somebody's hand, in a dark room, at ten at night.

0:00  arrive
Ten minutes
Put the phone down. It keeps time.
End

One instruction, once. Put it down. That is the only thing the person has to know, and it is also the sentence that makes the foreground rule feel like care rather than surveillance.

0:20  the light goes
End

The screen darkens itself over about twenty seconds. We deleted dark mode and this is the one place it was load-bearing: a cream screen at 10pm is a lamp in your face. Not a theme, a fade, and it reads as dusk falling rather than a setting. Otto dims with it.

4:12  you tapped
5:48
End

No number unless you ask. A visible countdown is a thing to watch, and watching it is the opposite of the exercise. Tap anywhere, it appears for three seconds, it goes. The ring is there the whole time for anyone who wants to know roughly without knowing exactly.

10:00  he opens his eyes
That's ten
Otto's fine for today.
Instagram is open again
Done

The light comes back up and Otto opens his eyes, which is the only animation that matters in the whole app. And the shield lifting is said out loud, because that is the thing the person traded for and they should be told they got it.

What happens if they leave the app. It pauses, it does not fail. Come back inside a couple of minutes and it picks up where it stopped; do not come back and the sit simply never finished. The only way to fail is to not return, and that is a decision rather than an accident. A session that punishes you for answering a text is a session people learn to be afraid of.

What is deliberately absent. No breathing pacer orb: Otto's chest is the pacer now that the rig breathes, and two things to follow is one too many. No live heart rate, ever, which is the oldest rule in this app. No music picker, no technique picker, no settings: every decision belongs before the timer starts, because each one is a dropout point and this screen is past the point of deciding.
