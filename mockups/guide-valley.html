Now they stand in the same valley as Home, Profile and the blocker editor: a band of sky and meadow at the top, grass below, white cards, sky blue for anything you pick or follow, and one gold button. Every word stays exactly as it is; the copy lives in `MeditationMethod.swift` and none of it changes.

The method's symbol sits on the seam, as a blocker's does in its editor. The steps are a numbered card with sky number dots, which reads as a sequence to follow rather than a paragraph to read. "Begin a session" is pinned at the bottom, gold, so it is there from the first step instead of at the end of the scroll.
