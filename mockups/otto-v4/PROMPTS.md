# Seven Ottos: what to paste into ChatGPT

Rewritten 2026-09-22 after the first four runs. What went wrong: the base
prompt said "heavy half-lowered eyelids", inherited from the old
old-and-wise brief, and it produced a sloth who looks stoned in every
healthy state. **Half-mast eyes are banned below.** Every state is either
properly open or properly closed, and the fur is richer and darker than the
washed-out runs.

Withered (1) came out right on the first try. Save it as
`aura-1-withered.png` and do not rerun it.

## Every run

- **Attach `reference-otto-sitting.png`.** For 2, also attach your approved
  withered image and say "between these two".
- **A new chat per prompt**, or the states blend into each other.
- Plain white background is fine, I cut it out.
- Save into this folder under the filename at the top of each prompt.

## The eye rule, since it is what broke

| # | eyes |
|---|------|
| 1 Withered | closed, or nearly |
| 2 Faded | open, looking down at the floor |
| 3 Stirring | open, round and clear, looking at you |
| 4 Steady | open, round and clear, calm |
| 5 Bright | open, round and clear, bright |
| 6 Radiant | smoothly and fully closed, meditating |
| 7 Nirvana | smoothly and fully closed, serene |

Never in between. A lid at half-mast reads as sleepy or high, which is the
one thing none of these may look like.

## Nudges, rather than starting a run over

- Stoned again: "his eyes are half closed, open them fully, round and clear,
  alert and calm."
- Washed out: "make the fur a deeper, richer caramel brown, less pale."
- Too young: "keep the eyes open but make the brow level and the face
  longer, he should read as an old animal."
- Closed eyes come out as slits at 6 or 7: "close his eyes completely, two
  smooth curved lines, no white showing."
- Too much light at 6 or 7: "keep the glow close to his body, the app draws
  the outer glow."
