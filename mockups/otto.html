Decisions in this mockup

  • Teal, not gold. Otto is guidance, so the mark is teal everywhere it appears. Each screen keeps one gold object: the send button in the chat, the unlock CTA on the locked screen, the ring on results.
  • The opening line is rules, not the model. It is built from the session's stored numbers with the verdict engine's own phrase bank, so it is instant, offline, and cannot contradict the results screen it sits under. Only the replies come from the model.
  • What Otto knows. A few hundred words we wrote: how the score is built (heart half, stillness, the breath doorway, the time ceiling), what the Watch can and cannot read, the guide's techniques, beginner basics. Plus a compact table of the last ten sessions (date, length, score, heart start and end, stillness, doorway, technique, rating). Nothing from the model's own training is trusted about 808.
  • What Otto never says. "The data suggests", never "you are". No diagnosis, no medical advice, no brainwaves, theta, HRV or health outcomes as facts about the person. Anything medical gets one line: "That one is for a doctor or another health professional, not for me." Medical questions are caught before the model sees them, and the model is told the same rule.
  • On the phone, by construction. Apple's on-device model; the app makes no network call. Analytics carry two names only, otto_opened and otto_asked: no question text, no number, ever.
  • Premium. Otto sorts under the standing rule: free gives you the score, paid gives you the evidence behind it. Otto is the evidence, spoken.
