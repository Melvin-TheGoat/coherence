What keeps you
coming back.

Each part of 808 is there to help you meditate again tomorrow.

Block

Otto holds your apps until you've meditated.

Pick the apps that pull you in and the hours to hold them. When you open one, Otto asks for five minutes first. Once you've meditated, they open again.

Otto's glow

Otto gets brighter as you keep going.

Each day you meditate, he gets a little brighter. Skip a few days and he starts to droop.

Meditate your way

Meditate the way you already like to.

Set a timer or keep it open. Play rain, a campfire, a tone, or our 25-minute guided journey. If you already have a meditation you like on YouTube or Spotify, start it and begin a session.

Rewards

See what each session added up to.

When a session ends you see your minutes, your streak and the points you earned. Points buy hats for Otto.

Progress

Track your streak and every session.

Your streak, every session you've done and the awards you've earned. One rest day a week won't break a streak.

Apple Watch

With an Apple Watch, see what your body did.

808 draws your heart rate, how still you were and your breathing, and gives the session a score out of 100. Without a Watch, everything else works the same.

What the
research says.

These are other researchers' studies, not ours. Here is what regular meditation has been shown to do.

Reading and focus+16 points

Two weeks of meditation training moved students 16 percentile points on GRE reading comprehension, in a randomized trial.2

Anxiety and depression3,515 people

Across 47 randomized trials, meditation produced real reductions in anxiety and depression.3

Regular practice280,000

Meditation sessions analyzed. People who meditated regularly improved more, and the length of each session didn't predict it.4

Where these numbers come from
  1. Killingsworth, M. A., & Gilbert, D. T. (2010). A wandering mind is an unhappy mind. Science, 330(6006), 932. doi:10.1126/science.1192439.
  2. Mrazek, M. D., Franklin, M. S., Phillips, D. T., Baird, B., & Schooler, J. W. (2013). Mindfulness training improves working memory capacity and GRE performance while reducing mind wandering. Psychological Science, 24(5), 776–781. doi:10.1177/0956797612459659.
  3. Goyal, M., et al. (2014). Meditation programs for psychological stress and well-being. JAMA Internal Medicine, 174(3), 357–368. doi:10.1001/jamainternmed.2013.13018. 47 trials with active controls, 3,515 participants. Moderate evidence, effect sizes around 0.3.
  4. Cearns, M., & Clark, S. R. (2023). The effects of dose, practice habits, and objects of focus on digital meditation effectiveness and adherence. Scientific Reports.

The research above shows that meditation practice helps. It does not show that 808 specifically will do any of this for you, and nothing here is medical advice. No one has studied 808 itself yet.
