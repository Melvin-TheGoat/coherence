Was that even working?

It's the question every meditation ends with — and the reason most people quit. With no way to see what happened inside, practice becomes an act of faith, and faith is easy to lose. So we made the invisible visible: while you meditate, your Watch quietly measures how your body responds, and afterward 808 shows you exactly what changed.

Three signals, measured on your wrist

  • Stillness — how deeply your body settled.
  • Heart rate — the downward drift as you relax.
  • Belly breathing — your breath slowing toward resonance (~6/min), recovered directly from the rise and fall of your wrist.
Requires iPhone + Apple Watch

808 is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and doesn't diagnose, treat, or provide medical advice. Your session results are computed on your devices and stay there.
