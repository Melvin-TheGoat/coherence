The meditation

The main event is a 25 minute guided session called The One Who Follows Through. It spends the first stretch bringing you into deep relaxation, the state where your brain shifts toward slower theta waves and the subconscious becomes directly reachable. That's the door. Once you're through it, the meditation has you live as the version of yourself who brings your goals to life, so the new identity embeds while your mind is actually open to it. Not picturing that person from the outside. Being them.

There are also frequency tones, nature sounds, and plain silence, if guided isn't your thing that day.

The proof

Most meditation apps ask you to take their word that anything happened. This one doesn't. During a session the Watch tracks how still you got and how your heart rate drifted. Lie down with the Watch resting on your belly and it reads your actual breathing too. Nothing is shown while you meditate. When you open your eyes, you get this:

A session from testing. The breathing number comes from lying down with the Watch resting on your belly, and it's optional.

Why these three numbers? Because they're the body's side of a theta state. In the deep meditative states where EEG studies find elevated theta activity, the body goes still, heart rate drifts down, and breathing settles toward six breaths a minute. We can't see brain waves from a wrist, so we measure everything your body does on the way there.
