808
Meditation for manifestation.

A guided-meditation app for iPhone & Apple Watch that shows you real biometric evidence your practice landed — measured on your wrist, shown after.

Most people meditate blind.

You close your eyes, follow your breath, and afterward wonder the same thing every time: was that working? 808 makes the invisible visible. While you meditate, your Apple Watch measures how your body responds — and afterward shows you the evidence, tracked session after session.

Three signals, measured on your wrist

  • Stillness — how deeply your body settled.
  • Heart rate — the downward drift as you relax.
  • Belly breathing — your breath slowing toward resonance (~6/min), recovered directly from motion.

808 is a wellness tool, not a medical device, and doesn't provide medical advice.
