This Privacy Policy explains how [LEGAL ENTITY NAME] ("we," "us," "808") handles information in the 808 app for iPhone and Apple Watch.

The short version

  • We don't run a server and can't see your data. Your account, sessions, and results live on your device; your account, preferences, and session log sync through your own private iCloud account (Apple's CloudKit). Your health results are stored only on the device that recorded them — never uploaded anywhere, not even iCloud. We have no backend that receives or stores your personal or health information.
  • We don't sell your data, run ads, or track you. No advertising identifiers, no third-party analytics, no cross-app tracking.
  • Your health data never leaves your control. It's measured on your Apple Watch and turned into your session results; we never use it for advertising and never share it.
  • You can delete everything anytime from Settings → Delete Account.

Information the app handles

Account information. When you use Sign in with Apple, the app receives and stores a unique Apple user identifier and — only if you choose to share them — your name and email (which may be an Apple private "Hide My Email" relay address). You may set a display name and choose whether to receive product emails.

Health and motion data. During a session your Apple Watch measures your heart rate (via Apple HealthKit) and movement (via the motion sensors) and processes them into your session results — a heart-rate trend (averaged, not beat-to-beat), a stillness measure, breathing metrics, and a summary score. The app requests HealthKit permission only for heart rate and workouts and reads your heart rate only live during a session; it does not read your broader Health history. We store the computed results, not raw biometric samples, and the iPhone reads no biometric data directly.

Session and app data. Session dates, durations, type, results and streak, and your preferences (theme, haptics, reminder time, default length).

What we do NOT collect. No location, contacts, photos, or browsing activity; no advertising identifiers; no third-party analytics or tracking.

Where your information lives

Your information lives in two places, by design. Health results stay on your device: session measurements (heart-rate trend, stillness, breathing) are stored only in the app's local storage on the device that recorded them — never synced to iCloud or anywhere else. Account and session log sync privately: your account info, preferences, and session log (dates, durations, types, ratings) sync to your personal private iCloud database via Apple's CloudKit. We do not operate servers that store your data and cannot access your private iCloud database. Apple processes this data under Apple's Privacy Policy.

How the information is used

  • To provide the app: run sessions, compute and show your evidence and history, and sync across your own devices.
  • To send a daily reminder, only if you enable it (a local notification on your device).
  • To send product emails, only if you opt in and provide a non-relay email; your address may be shared with our email delivery provider solely to send those messages. Opt out anytime.

We never use your health or motion data for advertising, and we never sell it.

Third parties

  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, HealthKit, CloudKit/iCloud sync, App Store.
  • Email delivery provider — only your email, and only if you opt into product emails.
  • Audio is bundled with the app; playing it sends no data about you.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data until you delete it. Settings → Delete Account signs you out and marks your account for deletion; it and all associated data are permanently removed within 30 days. Signing back in before then restores it.

Your choices and rights

Access and delete your data via Delete Account; opt in/out of product emails in Settings; control HealthKit permissions in iOS/watchOS settings. Depending on where you live (EU/UK GDPR, California CCPA/CPRA), you may have additional rights; because we hold no copy of your data on our servers, most requests are fulfilled through the app's deletion controls.

Consumer health data

This section supplements this policy for laws that specifically protect consumer health data (such as the Washington My Health My Data Act) and applies to all users. Categories: heart rate measured during a session you start; motion-derived measurements (stillness and, in belly-breathing sessions, breathing rate, depth, and regularity); and the session results computed from them. Source: your own Apple Watch's sensors, via Apple HealthKit and CoreMotion, only during a session you started. Purpose: solely to compute and show your own results. Consent: the app asks for your consent in-app before any measurement; Apple's HealthKit prompt independently controls heart-rate access. Sharing and sale: we do not sell consumer health data and do not share it — it is processed on your devices and we never receive it. Your rights: view results in the app; delete everything via Settings → Delete Account or by deleting the app from the device holding the results; contact [CONTACT EMAIL] with questions or requests.

Children

808 is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

Your data is protected by your device's security and Apple's iCloud encryption. No method is 100% secure, but operating no servers limits the attack surface to your device and Apple's infrastructure.
