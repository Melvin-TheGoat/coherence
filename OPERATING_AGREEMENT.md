## 1. Formation and purpose

1.1 The Company was formed under the Michigan Limited Liability Company Act
(the "**Act**") by Articles of Organization filed with LARA on [FILING DATE].

1.2 The Company's purpose is any lawful business, including developing,
publishing, and commercializing software products — initially the meditation
application marketed as "**808**" (the "**App**") — and any related content,
services, and brands.

1.3 The Company's principal place of business is [ADDRESS], or wherever the
Members designate. The registered agent is as stated in the Articles.

## 2. Membership interests

2.1 The Company has one class of membership interests, expressed as
percentage interests (the "**Interests**").

2.2 The Interests are: **Member A — 50%; Member B — 50%.**

2.3 No Member has priority over the other as to distributions, allocations,
or governance except as expressly stated here.

## 3. Capital contributions

3.1 As their initial capital contributions, each Member contributes:
(a) **$[100]** in cash, and (b) the **assigned intellectual property
described in Section 8**, which the Members agree has equal agreed value as
between them.

3.2 No Member is obligated to make further contributions. Additional
contributions require the written consent of both Members and, unless agreed
otherwise, are made equally.

3.3 No interest accrues on capital contributions; no Member may withdraw
capital except through distributions under Section 6.

## 4. Management

4.1 The Company is **member-managed**. Each Member has authority to conduct
ordinary day-to-day business (development, releases, marketing, routine
vendor purchases up to $[500] per transaction).

4.2 The following require the **unanimous written consent** of both Members
(email suffices):

  (a) selling, licensing (other than end-user licenses), or transferring the
      App or any material Company IP;
  (b) incurring debt or spending over $[1,000] in a single transaction or
      series of related transactions;
  (c) admitting a new Member or issuing new Interests;
  (d) hiring employees or engaging contractors over $[2,000];
  (e) distributions;
  (f) changing pricing/monetization of the App in a material way;
  (g) entering contracts longer than 12 months or outside the ordinary course;
  (h) merging, converting, dissolving, or filing bankruptcy;
  (i) amending the Articles or this Agreement;
  (j) settling or initiating litigation.

4.3 **Deadlock.** If the Members cannot agree on a Section 4.2 matter after
30 days of good-faith discussion, either Member may initiate mediation (video
conference acceptable). If mediation fails, the status quo continues — neither
Member may take the disputed action unilaterally.

## 5. Allocations

Profits and losses are allocated to the Members in proportion to their
Interests, subject to any allocations required by the Internal Revenue Code
and Treasury Regulations. The Company is intended to be taxed as a
partnership; the Members will cooperate on all tax elections. The
"partnership representative" under the Code is [Member A / Member B].

## 6. Distributions

6.1 Distributions are made when and as the Members unanimously agree, in
proportion to Interests.

6.2 **Tax distributions.** To the extent the Company has available cash, it
will endeavor to distribute annually at least enough for each Member to cover
income taxes attributable to Company allocations (estimated at a combined
rate of [40]%), pro rata.

## 7. Banking, records, and revenue

7.1 All Company revenue — including App Store proceeds, subscriptions, and any
platform payouts — is Company property and must be paid into a Company bank
account. Any Member who receives Company revenue personally (e.g., platform
payouts routed through an individual developer account **pending migration of
the App to a Company-owned account**) holds it in trust for the Company and
must remit it within 15 days.

7.2 The Members will migrate the App and its developer accounts (Apple
Developer Program, and any Meta or similar platform accounts) to
Company-owned organization accounts as soon as reasonably practicable.

7.3 Each Member has full access to Company books, accounts, credentials, and
records at all times.

## 8. Intellectual property assignment

8.1 **Assignment of existing work.** Each Member hereby irrevocably assigns
to the Company all right, title, and interest worldwide in and to all
intellectual property relating to the App and the 808 brand created by that
Member **before or on** the Effective Date, including: source code (including
the repositories currently under the Members' personal accounts), the signal
processing and analysis methods, designs, logos and marks (including "808"
and the mark artwork), guided-meditation scripts, commissioned narration and
audio (including all rights received from vendors), documentation, product
copy (including PURPOSE and SCIENCE materials), the website, and all
associated goodwill.

8.2 **Future work.** All IP relating to the Company's business created by a
Member while a Member is work made for the Company and is hereby assigned to
the Company on creation.

8.3 Each Member will execute any documents reasonably needed to perfect these
assignments (e.g., trademark or copyright filings) and appoints the Company
as attorney-in-fact to do so if the Member is unavailable.

8.4 **Third-party licenses** (e.g., commissioned narration, generated audio
beds) are held for the benefit of the Company; the Members will transfer or
re-paper them into the Company's name where the vendor permits.

## 9. Confidentiality and non-disparagement

Each Member will keep Company confidential information confidential, during
and after membership, except as required by law. Neither Member will
disparage the Company or the other Member publicly.

## 10. Transfers of Interests

10.1 No Member may transfer any Interest without the other Member's written
consent, except as provided in this Section.

10.2 **Right of first refusal.** A Member who receives a bona fide third-party
offer must first offer the Interest to the Company, then to the other Member,
on the same terms, each with 30 days to accept.

10.3 Any transferee who is not admitted as a Member by unanimous consent
receives economic rights only (no voting or management rights).

## 11. Departure, disability, death

11.1 **Voluntary departure.** A Member may withdraw from active involvement
on 60 days' written notice. A withdrawing Member retains their Interest but
their consent rights under Section 4.2 convert to consent rights only over
items (a), (c), (h), and (i) after 12 months of continuous inactivity.
*(Alternative for attorney discussion: a buyout at appraised value, or
time-based reverse vesting of Interests over [24] months from the Effective
Date. The Members should choose one model during legal review.)*

11.2 **Buyout option.** On a Member's death, permanent disability, or
withdrawal, the Company (first) and the other Member (second) have the option,
exercisable within 90 days, to purchase the departing Member's Interest at
fair market value determined by a neutral appraiser the parties select
jointly; payment may be made over up to 24 months with interest at the
applicable federal rate.

11.3 The estate or successor of a deceased Member receives economic rights
only unless admitted by consent.

## 12. Exculpation and indemnification

To the fullest extent permitted by the Act, no Member is liable to the
Company or the other Member for good-faith business judgments; the Company
indemnifies each Member against claims arising from authorized Company
activities, except for fraud, willful misconduct, or knowing violation of law.

## 13. Dissolution

The Company dissolves only on (a) unanimous written consent, or (b) as
required by the Act. On dissolution, assets go first to creditors, then to
Members in proportion to positive capital accounts / Interests. On
dissolution, each Member receives a perpetual, non-exclusive license to
jointly-owned Company IP only if the Members unanimously agree; otherwise
Company IP is sold or licensed for the account of both.

## 14. Dispute resolution

Any dispute arising out of this Agreement that cannot be resolved by
good-faith negotiation, then mediation, will be finally resolved by binding
arbitration in [Detroit, Michigan / remote by video], administered by the
American Arbitration Association under its rules, before a single arbitrator.
Judgment may be entered in any court of competent jurisdiction. Each party
bears its own costs unless the arbitrator awards otherwise.

## 15. General

15.1 **Governing law:** Michigan, without regard to conflicts rules.
15.2 **Entire agreement;** supersedes all prior discussions about ownership.
15.3 **Amendment:** unanimous written consent only.
15.4 **Counterparts / e-signature** permitted.
15.5 If any provision is unenforceable, the remainder stands.
