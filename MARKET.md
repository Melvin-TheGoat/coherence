# Market size, 2026-09-01

Estimated at Melvin's request while filling in App Store Connect. Every input
is labelled **sourced** or **assumed**, because our own standing rule is that a
number we cannot back is worse than no number. Attack the assumptions, not the
arithmetic.

**The binding constraint is the Apple Watch, not meditation.** That single
requirement is what makes 808's market different from every other meditation
app, in both directions: it shrinks the reachable population by roughly 85%,
and it is also the entire reason the product can make a claim nobody else can.

---

## The funnel

| Step | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Apple Watch active users, worldwide | **~170M** | **Sourced.** 2026 estimates; roughly half in the US. Counterpoint put the milestone at 100M earlier, so treat 170M as the optimistic end of a 100 to 170M band. |
| Share who meditate at least occasionally | **~15%** | **Assumed**, roughly US adult prevalence. Watch owners may skew higher (health-tracking, affluent), so this is probably conservative. |
| **TAM, people** | **~25M** | 170M x 15% |
| Blended revenue per paying user per year | **~$35** | **Assumed** from our own stack ($7.99/mo, $29.99/yr, $99.99 once). Category ARPU is ~$21, so we sit deliberately above it. |
| **TAM, revenue** | **~$875M/yr** | At 100% share, which is the definition of TAM and not a forecast. |

## The reality check that matters more than the TAM

**Calm, the category leader, has ~3.5M subscribers and its revenue FELL 24%
last year to ~$210M** (sourced, Business of Apps). The whole paying
meditation-app market is plausibly 8 to 12M subscribers worldwide across every
app that exists.

That number, not the TAM, is the one to hold in your head. It bounds everything.

| Step | Value | Basis |
|---|---|---|

| Share of those who own an Apple Watch | **~33%** | **Assumed.** Skews high versus the general population for the same reasons as above. |

## SOM

A well-executed indie app taking **1 to 3% of SAM over three years** is **30k to
120k subscribers, roughly $1M to $4M ARR**.

For calibration rather than comparison: Athlytic is a solo developer doing
essentially this motion on the fitness side (Apple Watch data in, a 0-100
readiness score out) with ~11K ratings at 4.8. Gentler Streak is a small indie
team with an Apple Design Award. Neither is a unicorn and both are real
businesses.

---

## What the numbers actually tell us

**1. The Watch gate costs about 85% of the market, and the camera branch is the
expansion play.** A phone-only competitor addresses everyone; we address Watch
owners. That is the price of the differentiator and it is worth paying at this
stage, because it is also the moat. But it means `camera-vision` (parked, pilot
POSITIVE on two real videos) is not a nice-to-have experiment. It is the single
largest lever on market size we have, worth roughly a 6x on the addressable
base, and it should be evaluated as a market decision rather than a feature.

**2. Calm's decline is the opening.** A shrinking leader with falling
subscribers means the "more guided content" model is saturated. Nobody in this
category is winning on measurement, which is the one thing we do.

**3. Our pricing sits above category ARPU on purpose**, which is defensible
because the Watch requirement already selects for people who spend on health
hardware. The consequence is that we compete on demonstrated value and can
never win a discount war. That is consistent with everything else about the
product, and it is the reason the paywall shows evidence before it asks.

**4. The realistic near-term ceiling is a few million ARR, not a venture
outcome, on the current addressable base.** Worth saying plainly, because it
sets what kind of company this is unless the Watch gate comes off.
